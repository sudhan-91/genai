---
name: product
description: >
  Query product master data and BOM: the static attributes of a product or part number. Use
  whenever the user asks what a product is, where it is made, or what it is made of, even
  if they never say "master data". Covers: manufacturing sites and country of origin (wafer
  fab, wafer test, assembly, final test); package, division, PL, quality category, process
  group/technology; quality milestone, QA status, M9/mass-production release date, Pre-M9;
  MSL, ESD class (CDM/HBM), shelf life/storage days, dry pack; RoHS, halogen-free,
  lead-free, HFGR; packing type and unit; product/material status (active, discontinued);
  BOM/construction: body, die and wafer size, die thickness, moulding compound, bonding
  wire, die attach, leadframe, plating, net weight, technology node, test temperatures,
  marking. Look-ups by sales name, OPN, SP, MA or BAU number, Basic Type, or filters such
  as "products in PL X". Do NOT use for complaints/FAR/returns, test results or yield,
  sales, orders, or inventory, even when a product is named.
---

---
name: far
description: >
  Query customer complaint data: FAR (Failure Analysis Report), CMA cases, and PSI returned
  items. Use whenever the user asks about complaints, customer returns, field failures, or
  failure analysis cases, even if they never say "FAR". Covers: case counts by product,
  customer, product line (PL), business group, region, or period; root cause breakdown
  (EOS/ESD/EIPD, MOS, TOS, NTF; combined, level 1, level 2); customer-caused vs Infineon
  (IFX)-caused share; cause owner (site, dept, subcon); PSI failure description, keyword,
  and quantity; corrective actions; TAT; user status; start, completion, and registration
  dates. Look-ups by CMA/case number, MA number, sales name, package, product process, BE
  lot number, datecode, or complainer name/country. Do NOT use for sales, pricing,
  shipments, orders, inventory/WIP, test yield or parametric data, BOM/design data, product
  specs, or qualification/reliability stress tests unrelated to a complaint. Post-shipment
  complaint and return data only.
---

---
name: shipment
description: >
  Query shipment and delivery records: which product and lot was shipped, how many units,
  to whom, from where, and when. Use whenever the user asks about shipments, deliveries,
  shipped quantities, tracking, or who the customers of a product are, even if they never
  say "shipment". Covers: shipped quantity and lot counts by product, customer, country, or
  period; lot number and lot datecode (YYWW) traceability; ship-from location; delivery
  date; AWB tracking number; dispatch/delivery note number; sold-to customer (name, number,
  country: the order owner) and ship-to recipient (name, city, country: may differ from
  sold-to, e.g. EMS or drop-ship). Look-ups by sales name, SP or MA number, package,
  division, PL, lot, datecode, AWB, dispatch note, or customer name. Do NOT use for product
  specs, BOM, or manufacturing sites (product skill); complaints, returns, or root cause
  (far skill); pricing, revenue, or order data; test or yield data; inventory or stock,
  even when a lot or customer is named.
---
